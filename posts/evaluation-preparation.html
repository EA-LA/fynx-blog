1. Read the account-specific rules

Save the rules that apply to the program you select. Check the target, minimum trading requirements, permitted instruments and strategy restrictions. Do not assume that another program’s rules apply to this one.

2. Understand how losses are measured

Identify whether the program measures balance, equity or both, and whether a limit is static or trailing. Check the daily reset time and whether open positions, commissions or other charges count toward the limit.

3. Write a session plan

Decide which setups you intend to trade and which conditions will make you pause. A written plan gives your post-session review something concrete to compare against.

4. Review the full program conditions

Check fees, eligibility, account type and any conditions governing rewards or payouts. Understand what happens after an evaluation and what happens if a rule is breached. Ask support about anything unclear before purchasing.

5. Make review part of the routine

Keep a record of the trade idea, execution and whether the plan was followed. Review deviations even when a trade ends positively. A profitable outcome by itself does not show that the decision was repeatable.
