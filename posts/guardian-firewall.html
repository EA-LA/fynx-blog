Put behavior into the review

Guardian is part of FYNX’s risk-focused product story. The underlying idea is straightforward: review how you trade, not just whether the last trade won or lost.

Watch for changes in the routine

Changes in position size, frequency or willingness to follow a plan can be useful prompts to pause. A series of wins or losses can influence the next decision, even when the market setup has not changed.

A simple review loop

  • Before the session, record the plan and the conditions that require a pause.
  • After each trade, note whether the plan was followed.
  • At the weekly review, identify repeated deviations and a concrete adjustment.
