Inside the FYNX calculators stack

Position size, margin and pip value: understanding the inputs behind a useful calculation.

Start with the inputs

A trading calculator is useful only when its inputs match the instrument and account. Confirm the account currency, contract size, price increment and leverage assumptions before relying on a result.

Give each calculation a purpose

Position-size calculations connect a planned stop distance with an amount at risk. Margin calculations estimate the collateral required to hold exposure. Pip-value calculations help interpret a price movement in account-currency terms. These are related questions, but they answer different parts of a trade plan.

Check against your platform

Compare the output with your trading platform’s instrument specifications. Costs, execution, exchange rates and broker settings can change the actual result. A calculator supports a decision; it cannot guarantee an execution price or a loss limit.
