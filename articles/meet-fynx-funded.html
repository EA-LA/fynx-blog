A closer look at FYNX Funded

An introduction to the funded side of the FYNX ecosystem, and what to review before choosing an evaluation.

The funded side of FYNX

FYNX Funded gives the FYNX ecosystem a dedicated home for trading evaluations. The platform brings challenge selection, program rules and account resources into a focused experience. Alongside FYNX Finance World, it connects two parts of a trader’s routine: the tools used to prepare and review, and the requirements of an evaluation.

Start with the program, then the account size

An account’s headline size is only one part of the decision. The evaluation structure, loss calculations, permitted strategies and payout conditions deserve the same attention. Compare the current options on FYNX Funded before choosing a program.

Use the program’s published terms as your reference. Pricing, availability and conditions can change; this introduction is not a replacement for the terms attached to an account.

A process you can repeat

Before the first session, put the relevant rules somewhere you will actually check them. Record the reset time, what counts toward a loss limit and the conditions that require you to stop. After the session, review the decisions behind the result as well as the result itself.

Explore the platform

Visit FYNX Funded for current evaluation options and program information. For the tools side of the ecosystem, explore FYNX Finance World.
