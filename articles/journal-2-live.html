Building a useful trading journal

A practical approach to documenting decisions and reviewing the process behind each trade.

A record you can learn from

A useful trading journal keeps the reasoning behind a trade alongside its outcome. Record the setup, intended invalidation point and what actually happened during execution.

Review the process

Use consistent labels for setups and mistakes so that sessions can be compared. Separate a planned loss from a decision that ignored the plan. Both may look the same in an account statement, but they require different responses.

Keep a weekly review

Look for repeated deviations and choose a concrete adjustment for the next week. Keep that adjustment small enough to assess in the following review.
